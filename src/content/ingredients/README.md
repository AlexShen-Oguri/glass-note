# Independent ingredient catalogue

`records.json` contains a curated subset of the Open Food Facts food ingredient taxonomy. It is independent of the cocktail recipe list. `translations.json` contains editorial name translations and corrections; they are drafts, not translations verified by the original source or professional reviewers.

Source: Open Food Facts contributors, revision `6a109357c9b9b07b713e1df702f79e6fcdb576e2`, [ingredients taxonomy](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/6a109357c9b9b07b713e1df702f79e6fcdb576e2/taxonomies/food/ingredients.txt) plus ten named materials from the [additive taxonomy](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/6a109357c9b9b07b713e1df702f79e6fcdb576e2/taxonomies/additives.txt). Each additive remains one ingredient; E-number variants are not counted separately. Its search aliases are restricted to reviewed names and the source food additive identifier, because the wider source synonym lists include salts and ambiguous legacy chemical names.

Upstream documentation at the same revision: [server overview](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/6a109357c9b9b07b713e1df702f79e6fcdb576e2/README.md) and [taxonomy overview](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/6a109357c9b9b07b713e1df702f79e6fcdb576e2/taxonomies/README.md).

Each record retains its original name, source line, record identifier, aliases and taxonomy parents. The app links to the pinned source and license both in the library and in individual profiles. Editorial illustration families and use notes are separate from source facts. Original ingredient illustrations are drawn in `scripts/build-ingredient-art.mjs`; no Open Food Facts product photos are included.

Selection inputs live in [the maintained source snapshot](../../../research/ingredients/open-food-facts/source-selection.json). It retains the pinned source metadata, selection policies, original record fields and order of the 1,096 selected taxonomy records. The [bar-technique inputs](../../../research/ingredients/open-food-facts/bar-technique-materials.json) retain the ten additive records. The broader unselected taxonomy drafts are not maintained, and no script claims to reproduce their original selection from upstream. `scripts/build-ingredient-library.ts` applies the maintained selections and identity merge rules. Synonyms are merged; meaningful ingredient forms and named varieties remain distinct. Ancestry, illustrations and aliases never establish recipe substitutions, exact brand ownership or verified composition. New independent ingredients have unknown composition and no fabricated recipe links.

The list covers bar ingredients and a broad culinary shelf. It is not a claim that every common material worldwide has been captured. Regenerate the material list first, then merge [the ordered translation overlays](../../../research/ingredients/translation-overlays.json) with `scripts/merge-ingredient-translations.mjs`. The overlay retains source-derived and editorial labels and explicitly records superseded values; all runtime overlay names remain drafts. Generated worksheets, accepted names and reports go to ignored `artifacts/ingredients/`. See [the maintenance commands](../../../research/README.md).

The runtime catalogue combines recipe materials with independently selected taxonomy additions. Count the current integrated catalogue rather than treating archived source-selection counts as product totals. Illustration families are reusable form/category cues, not individual drawings for every ingredient. Eight display-name fields are supplied, but editorial translations and specialist terminology still require professional native-language review; original names remain available in profiles.
