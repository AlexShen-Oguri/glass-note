# Independent ingredient catalogue

`records.json` contains a curated subset of the Open Food Facts food ingredient taxonomy. It is independent of the cocktail recipe list. `translations.json` contains editorial name translations and corrections; they are drafts, not translations verified by the original source or professional reviewers.

Source: Open Food Facts contributors, revision `6a109357c9b9b07b713e1df702f79e6fcdb576e2`, [ingredients taxonomy](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/6a109357c9b9b07b713e1df702f79e6fcdb576e2/taxonomies/food/ingredients.txt) plus ten named materials from the [additive taxonomy](https://github.com/openfoodfacts/openfoodfacts-server/blob/6a109357c9b9b07b713e1df702f79e6fcdb576e2/taxonomies/additives.txt). Each additive remains one ingredient; E-number variants are not counted separately. Its search aliases are restricted to reviewed names and the source food additive identifier, because the wider source synonym lists include salts and ambiguous legacy chemical names.

Each record retains its original name, source line, record identifier, aliases and taxonomy parents. The app links to the pinned source and license both in the library and in individual profiles. Editorial illustration families and use notes are separate from source facts. Original ingredient illustrations are drawn in `scripts/build-ingredient-art.mjs`; no Open Food Facts product photos are included.

Selection and identity merge rules are reproducible in `scripts/build-ingredient-library.ts`, with explicit selections and review records under `research/ingredients/product/`. Synonyms are merged; meaningful ingredient forms and named varieties remain distinct. Ancestry, illustrations and aliases never establish recipe substitutions, exact brand ownership or verified composition. New independent ingredients have unknown composition and no fabricated recipe links.

The list covers bar ingredients and a broad culinary shelf. It is not a claim that every common material worldwide has been captured. Regenerate the material list first, then merge the translation overlays with `scripts/merge-ingredient-translations.mjs`.

Current integrated coverage, read from the runtime catalogue on 2026-09-16: **1,367 ingredients**. This combines recipe materials with the independently selected taxonomy additions; later recipe expansions add their materials too. Illustration families are reusable form/category cues, not 1,367 individual drawings. Eight display-name fields are supplied, but editorial translations and specialist terminology still require professional native-language review; original names remain available in profiles. Earlier selection and draft-count reports under `research/ingredients/` are dated evidence, not current coverage totals.
