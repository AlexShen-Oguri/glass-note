# Bottle product images

`manifest.json` and `src/content/bottle-media.ts` map each bottle to its original and delivery files, source page, asset URL, visual observations, packaging notes and hashes. `originals/` preserves source bytes; `display/` contains proportionally resized delivery images. No generated substitute is used for product labels or bottle shapes.

The UI's framing bounds centre complete bottles without changing image pixels. Re-review these bounds when replacing an image. A source image from a different market edition does not establish the alcohol strength of a user's own bottle. Images are not recipe or flavour evidence.

These are third-party assets, excluded from the application-code MIT licence. Public availability does not grant redistribution rights. Unconfirmed permission remains unconfirmed even when the image is included in a public repository, website or installer. Preserve the attribution and review the applicable source terms before reuse.
